# Interview Synthesizer

Turns interview transcripts in input/ into themes and a one page memo.
All data in input/ is synthetic.

## Case
Client: Northfield Markets (fictional regional grocer, 120 stores)
Question: Should Northfield double its private label share in the
next two years, and what would have to be true?

## Topics (use only these tags)
Customer demand, Quality and brand perception, Margin and pricing,
Supplier capacity, Store operations, Competition, Other
A sensible option: When a claim fits two topics, tag the root cause, not the symptom.

## Rules
* Quotes are always verbatim. Never fix grammar inside a quote.
* Every claim cites an interview id, like [INT03].
* Never invent a quote or a number.
* Never edit files in input/.
