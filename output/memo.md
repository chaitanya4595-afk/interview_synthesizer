# Northfield Markets: Should we double private label?
**Answer:** Grow private label, but replace the two-year chain-wide doubling with targets that flex by store cluster and category, starting where demand, supply and margin align [INT02, INT03, INT05, INT08]. This requires fixing weak items, refreshing packaging, keeping a real price gap and the brands shoppers love, reliable stock, a category-by-category rollout, easier supplier payment terms, margin modeled net of lost trade funds, and funding for launch capacity, reset labor, trial offers and a premium tier [INT01-INT08].

## A staged target will beat a chain-wide doubling in two years.
INT08 calls it "a stretch in two years" needing "roughly 400 new items" plus resets in every store [INT08]. INT03 calls it "a headquarters number, not a store number" [INT03]. INT04 disagrees: "In dry grocery, I think it's realistic if they start now and they commit" [INT04].

## Fix quality and the "cheap" image before adding shelf space.
All eight interviews agree. "You can't double share with a brand people are embarrassed to put in their cart" [INT08]. "You lose somebody on one product and they don't try the next one" [INT01].

## Lead with dry grocery, because dairy, frozen and fresh supply is tight.
INT04 reports about 70% utilization; INT05 runs "close to 90% most of the year" and says doubling there "is not realistic unless they bring in more suppliers" [INT05]. INT04 wants "a multi-year agreement with minimum volumes" first [INT04].

## Pass savings to shoppers and model margin net of lost trade funds.
INT08 cites 32 to 35% gross margin versus about 25% for brands, but expects to "lose a meaningful part of trade funds" [INT08]. INT02 warns: "The price gap has to be real, like 20 to 30% under the brand, not five cents" [INT02].

## Add store brand next to loved brands, starting in value-focused stores.
"If I take their ketchup off the shelf, I'm giving them a reason to leave too" [INT03]. Demand is uneven: INT02 has "watched people trade down in real time", while INT01 says "People here don't really shop us on price, honestly" [INT01].

## What we still need to find out
* How much trade funding is at risk, which could offset the margin gain [INT08].
* Whether fresh and frozen suppliers beyond INT05 can scale, which decides where to grow [INT08].
* Which categories meet demand, supply and margin tests [INT08].
