# Northfield Markets: Should we double private label?
**Answer:** Grow private label, but not by doubling chain-wide in two years; stage it by category and store cluster, led by dry grocery [INT03, INT04, INT08]. It requires better quality and packaging, supplier volume commitments, and a visible price gap that keeps loyal brands [INT02, INT03, INT04, INT08].

## Doubling chain-wide in two years is a stretch, so we should stage growth by category and store cluster.
It needs "roughly 400 new items, a full packaging redesign, significant supplier investment, and store resets across every store" [INT08]. Stores see "a headquarters number, not a store number" [INT03]. The one dissent is category-specific: "In dry grocery, I think it's realistic if they start now and they commit." [INT04]

## We must fix the brand's "cheap" image before adding items.
"You can't double share with a brand people are embarrassed to put in their cart." [INT08] The fix is specs and packaging: "Honestly, quality is usually a spec problem, not a manufacturing problem." [INT04]

## Suppliers can grow dry grocery now, but dairy and frozen need new suppliers and firm volume commitments.
Dry grocery lines run at "about 70% utilization right now, so there's room." [INT04] Dairy runs "close to 90%" [INT05]. Suppliers want "a multi-year agreement with minimum volumes" [INT04].

## The margin prize holds only if shoppers see a real price gap and we net out lost trade funds.
Store brand earns "around 32 to 35%, compared to about 25% on the comparable national brands" [INT08], but the gap must be "20 to 30% under the brand, not five cents." [INT02] Northfield "could lose a meaningful part of trade funds" [INT08].

## We should add store-brand options alongside loyal brands, not pull them, or shoppers will leave.
"We're kind of stuck in the middle if we don't move." [INT02] Yet "If I take their ketchup off the shelf, I'm giving them a reason to leave too." [INT03]

## What we still need to find out
* Net margin by category: estimates run from "roughly 8 points more" [INT02] to "a few points lower than they hoped" [INT04].
* Which stores go first: shoppers "trade down in real time" [INT02], while "People here don't really shop us on price, honestly." [INT01]
* Dairy quality: [INT02] calls it "as good as the brand"; [INT05] and [INT01] describe a yogurt problem that stopped trial.
